Our Story

We first met in grade eleven on Vancouver Island. Neither of us could have known then just how our journey together would unfold.

For the past 12 years, a little string has woven its way through our lives, always connecting us back to each other despite distance and time.

Looking back, we feel incredibly grateful for every twist and turn, every place, and every moment that brought us closer to this life together. We are now thankful to live together in Hamilton, Ontario with our sweet dog Morita.

And now, we are so excited to begin our next chapter — surrounded by the people we love most.
