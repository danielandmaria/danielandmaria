Frequently Asked Questions

We’ll keep this page updated as plans come together.

When and where is the wedding?

Our wedding is on November 7, 2027 at The Serene House in Luquillo, Puerto Rico.

What time does the wedding begin?

Ceremony, cocktail hour, and dinner & dancing times are currently TBD. We’ll update the website as soon as the schedule is finalized.

What is the dress code?

Tropical Formal. We’re encouraging vibrant greens, sunset oranges, soft yellows, bold pinks, and blush tones.

For men, suits or more relaxed formal attire are encouraged in darker tones such as navy, black, or deep green. Please avoid beige or tan suits.

Which airport should I fly into?

Fly into San Juan International Airport. The airport is approximately 60 minutes from the venue.

When should I arrive in Puerto Rico?

We recommend arriving on November 5th or 6th, ahead of the wedding on November 7th. Our families will likely be arriving Thursday evening, and some guests may choose to stay for a full week and enjoy Puerto Rico as a vacation destination.

Where should I stay?

Please hold off on booking accommodations for now. We are working on setting up a hotel block, hopefully with affordable rates for everyone.

There are also a few beachfront resorts and smaller boutique hotels within approximately 10–20 minutes of the venue.

How do I RSVP?

Use the RSVP form on our wedding website. Your response will be added directly to our private wedding RSVP list.
